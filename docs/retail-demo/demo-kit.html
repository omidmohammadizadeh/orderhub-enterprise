Corner Grocer — demo kit

Everything to show a shop owner OrderHub's till, online shop and picking in ten minutes. Print the barcodes (page 2 onwards) and scan them like real stock.

Before the demo (5 minutes, once)

  1. Add the shop. Dashboard → Locations → Add location → choose Grocery. Name it “Corner Grocer (demo)”, set opening hours, and give it an online ordering link.
  2. Load the products. Stock & barcodes → Import spreadsheet → pick corner-grocer-demo.csv. 51 products arrive with prices, stock, cost prices, barcodes, age limits and weighed items, on a “Shop” menu for the till and online.
  3. Scale labels. Stock & barcodes → Label scale → leave it on “Price in the barcode (5 digits)”. That's the layout of the labels on the last page.
  4. Create the offers in Marketing → New campaign → Multi-buy (below). Tick both Till and Online shop.

The demo (10 minutes)

  1. Scan a basket. On the till, scan Coca-Cola and three bags of Walkers. The basket shows “3 for £2.50” without anyone pressing anything.
  2. Meal deal. Scan a sandwich, a chocolate bar and a Diet Coke: the meal deal applies and beats the crisps offer when both could.
  3. Challenge 25. Scan the Stella. The till stops and asks for an ID check; show “Refuse sale” and then “Customer is 18+”. The check is recorded on the order.
  4. Loose and deli. Tap Bananas and type 0.642 kg on the keypad. Then scan the Wiltshire Ham scale label: price straight from the label.
  5. Take payment. Cash or card; the receipt prints with a QR code.
  6. Return. Press Returns, scan the receipt QR, return one item: refunded and back in stock.
  7. Online shop. On a phone, open the shop link. Add 1 kg of bananas (“weighed when we pick it”), crisps and the wine. The basket asks “I'm 18 or over” and shows the multi-buy saving.
  8. Pick it. Dashboard → Picking: aisle by aisle. Weigh the bananas at 930 g: the screen shows the refund for the missing 70 g. Finish, and the order is ready.
  9. Stock. Stock & barcodes: every sale, return and re-weigh has already moved the counts. Download the stock report for the value at cost.

Tip: open the storefront on your own phone before the meeting so the page is warm, and keep the till on a tablet with a USB or Bluetooth scanner.
