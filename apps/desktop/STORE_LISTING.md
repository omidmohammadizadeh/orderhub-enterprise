## Short description

## Description
Order Hub Solutions is the all-in-one EPOS for restaurants, takeaways and multi-brand kitchens. Run your counter till, see every online order in one place and manage your menus from the same Windows app.

TAKE ORDERS AT THE COUNTER
• Fast touch-friendly POS for collection, delivery and dine-in
• Cash, card machine and pay-on-collection payments
• Keeps taking cash orders if the internet drops, then syncs automatically

EVERY CHANNEL, ONE SCREEN
• Just Eat, Deliveroo, Uber Eats and your own website orders arrive together
• A sound alert for every new order, with auto-print to your receipt printer
• Kitchen display, ticket numbers and order history

MENUS AND MARKETING
• One menu for every channel, with per-channel prices and items marked unavailable in one tap
• Branded online ordering website and customer app
• Promo codes, loyalty, email and SMS campaigns

BUILT FOR SHOP COUNTERS
• Opens full-screen and keeps the screen awake through a shift
• Prints to USB, Bluetooth and network receipt printers
• Stays signed in between restarts

An Order Hub Solutions account is required. Sign up at www.orderhubsolutions.com.
